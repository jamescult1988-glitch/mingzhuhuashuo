# Privacy Policy · 隐私政策

Last updated / 更新日期: 2026-11-05

## English

"Mingzhu Huashuo Publisher" is a private tool used only by the owner of the 名著华说 (Mingzhu Huashuo) channels to publish his own videos, upload his own subtitles and read the statistics of his own channels on YouTube, Facebook, Instagram and Threads.

- **Data accessed:** only data of the owner's own channels (videos, captions, comments, view statistics), through the official APIs, with the owner's explicit consent.
- **Use:** to publish and schedule the owner's episodes and to produce internal performance reports. Nothing else.
- **Sharing:** data is never sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.
- **Storage:** access tokens are stored encrypted (GitHub Actions secrets). Statistics are kept only as internal reports.
- **Other users:** the tool does not collect any data about viewers or other users.
- **Revocation:** access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions and in Facebook / Instagram settings.
- **YouTube:** this tool uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service (https://www.youtube.com/t/terms) and the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **Contact:** open an issue at https://github.com/jamescult1988-glitch/mingzhuhuashuo/issues

## 中文

"Mingzhu Huashuo Publisher" 是「名著华说」频道主本人使用的私人工具，仅用于在 YouTube、Facebook、Instagram 和 Threads 上发布自己的视频、上传自己的字幕、查看自己频道的数据。

- 只读取频道主本人账号的数据（视频、字幕、评论、播放数据），并经本人明确授权。
- 数据不出售、不与第三方共享、不用于广告。
- 不收集观众或其他用户的任何个人数据。
- 可随时在 Google 账号权限页面及 Facebook / Instagram 设置中撤销授权。
